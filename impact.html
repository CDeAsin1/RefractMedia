Research & Projects

🔬 Independent Research Paper
Correlating Genetic and Neuroanatomical Markers to Gender Dysphoria: Analysis of the Relationship of Androgen Receptor CAG Repeat Length and Somatostatin Neuronal Density to the Transfeminine Brain
Author: Cristóbal de Asín · 2026 · Methodology: literature synthesis across 6 peer-reviewed studies · APA 7th edition

This paper examines the neurobiological basis of gender dysphoria by analysing the relationship between androgen receptor CAG repeat polymorphisms and somatostatin neuronal density in the bed nucleus of the stria terminalis (BSTc) in the transfeminine brain. The study synthesises findings from six peer-reviewed studies in neuroendocrinology and neuroanatomy, applying a correlational framework to evaluate the extent to which genetic markers predict neuroanatomical variation associated with gender identity. The research directly engages with the scientific literature that lawmakers and media routinely ignore when reporting on LGBTQ+ policy.

How We Measure Impact

METHOD 01
Audience Engagement

Tracking readership, newsletter subscribers, and social media reach across Instagram and TikTok. We measure not just views, but return visits and shares — indicators that content is genuinely useful, not just consumed once.

METHOD 02
Student Interviews

Qualitative research with young people on media literacy habits, trust in news sources, and awareness of LGBTQ+ rights coverage. Interviews are ongoing and will be published here as they are completed and consented.

METHOD 03
Research Output

The founder's neuroanatomy research paper directly extends Refract's science-backed approach to LGBTQ+ coverage. When published, it will be the first original research by the platform and a benchmark for the outlet's editorial credibility.

METHOD 04
Institutional Projects

PAS documents how Refract's research methodology — rigorous sourcing, structured investigation, transparent findings — translates from journalism into direct institutional change at the school level.
